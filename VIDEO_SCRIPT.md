# PatchNotes for Learning · Demo Script

Target length: 3–4 minutes. Record the real browser interaction; do not use a slideshow as a substitute.

## 0:00–0:30 · Problem

“A beginner can make a change and still not know how to explain what they learned. An assistant can write a polished story, but that story may not belong to the author. PatchNotes for Learning keeps the evidence visible and keeps the reflection with the person who made the change.”

## 0:30–1:30 · Input and evidence

Open the live demo and click **Load sample**. Point out that the sample is a unified diff and that the app stays browser-only. Explain the visible file name, added lines, removed lines, and hunk count. The counts come from parsing the diff, not from a generated summary.

## 1:30–2:45 · Reflection

Write a short answer in both reflection fields. Explain that the prompts ask what changed and what was learned or tested. Click **Mark reflection written** and point to the status change. The app records that the author supplied the text; it does not grade or rewrite the claim.

## 2:45–3:30 · Design boundary

“This is intentionally smaller than an AI tutor or code reviewer. It does not read a local repository, upload code, call an API, or invent a learning story. The diff is the evidence, and the author owns the explanation.”

## 3:30–3:45 · Disclosure

“The project was drafted with AI coding assistance. The implementation and documentation were reviewed by the project owner, who remains responsible for understanding and explaining the code.”
